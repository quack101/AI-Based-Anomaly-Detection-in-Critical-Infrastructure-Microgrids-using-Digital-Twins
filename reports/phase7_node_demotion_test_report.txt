----------------------------------------------------------------------
Why demoting MORE nodes made things WORSE, not just unhelpful
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A plausible mechanism, consistent with reports/
phase7_solver_instability_fix_report.txt's own root-cause finding:
demoting a node from a HARD constraint to a WEIGHTED pseudo-measurement
does not remove it from the problem -- it moves 78 rows from the
p=250 constraint block into the m=304 weighted block, which the SAME
null-space Gauss-Newton solve must still fit via `w` (the tangential,
measurement-fit step). That solver step was ALREADY shown (reports/
phase7_solver_instability_fix_report.txt Step 1e) to be inadequately
damped and comparably oversized to the constraint-correcting step at
these exact electrically-sensitive buses. Moving MORE rows into the
block `w` must fit, with the SAME under-damped construction, gives `w`
MORE (now-weighted, not hard) residual to chase at exactly the nodes
already causing trouble -- worsening, not relieving, the pressure on
an already-struggling tangential step, rather than removing a source
of infeasibility the hard-constraint framing had been causing. This is
consistent with -- not contradictory to -- all five prior reports'
finding that the SOLVER's step construction (not the constraint set)
is the proximate cause of the non-convergence; moving constraints
around without fixing that construction does not help, and in this
case measurably hurts.

FINAL SUMMARY -- SIX INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATIONS, SIX REPORTS
======================================================================
This report closes a six-report investigation into Phase 7's original
"100% convergence, mean 1.00 iterations" claim, each probing a
genuinely different hypothesis:

  1. reports/phase7_convergence_remediation_report.txt -- audited the
     ORIGINAL convergence criterion (step-size-only) for circularity
     and looseness. Found: not circular; too loose (masks a ~100-200
     kvar hard-constraint violation at declared "convergence").
     RETRACTED the original claim.

  2. reports/phase7_solver_instability_fix_report.txt -- root-caused,
     via direct single-iteration instrumentation, WHY the corrected
     (combined) criterion still fails: a shared global trust-region
     clip conflates the constraint-correcting (y_particular) and
     measurement-fit (Z@w) step components, letting the latter's
     irrelevant size dilute the former's otherwise-exact correction.
     THREE fix attempts (symmetric LM damping, a naive trust-region
     clip, a textbook Byrd-Omojokun split) all failed or worsened the
     targeted case. DID NOT COMMIT.

  3. reports/phase7_accept_criterion_fix_report.txt -- tested whether
     the ACCEPT/REJECT test itself (binary merit-improvement, not a
     calibrated trust-region ratio) was the deeper defect. Implemented
     the standard N&W ch.4 ratio test exactly as specified; failed its
     own cheap single-point check (WORSE, not better). DID NOT COMMIT.

  4. reports/phase7_iteration_cap_test_report.txt -- tested whether
     the REALISTIC (non-adversarial) stratified sample simply needed
     more iterations. Raised the cap 30->150: 0/6 pilot points moved
     AT ALL -- every point is a genuine fixed point reached well before
     iteration 30, confirmed bit-identical at 150 as at 30. DID NOT
     raise the cap.

  5. THIS report -- tested whether the constraint SET (not the solver)
     was the problem, by demoting the ~14 switch-adjacent nodes the
     solver-instability report's diagnostic surfaced, using the
     IDENTICAL mechanism already shipped for the 15 regulator-adjacent
     nodes. 0/6 pilot points improved; ALL 6 got WORSE. DID NOT COMMIT.

NONE of the five code-level investigations (2-5) found a committable
fix. The underlying WLS/null-space/hard-constraint FORMULATION itself
(Part A of the original Phase 7 report) has not been challenged by any
of these reports -- only the specific numerical solve, and now the
constraint-set partition, have been probed and found wanting from five
independent angles. estimation/state_estimator.py and observability/
zero_injection.py are BOTH reverted and confirmed byte-identical to
their state before this six-report series began. No further local

reports/phase7_solver_instability_fix_report.txt's Step 4 names three

contraction evidence -- tested and falsified by report 4; or a fully

This is the honest, complete state of the investigation: six
independently-motivated hypotheses tested, six negative or
insufficient results, each documented with hard numeric evidence
rather than guessed at or glossed over.
