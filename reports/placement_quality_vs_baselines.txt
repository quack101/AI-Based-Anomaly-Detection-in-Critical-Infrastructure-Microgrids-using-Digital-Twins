1. ANSWERS TO THE THREE SPECIFIC QUESTIONS ASKED
========================================================================

Q1 -- Does the branch-diversity knee (opens ~30, closes ~85) hold in
      BOTH constructions?
  PARTIALLY, with a real difference in WHERE it opens:
    independent: opens at count=30 (13.7% ahead, clears mean+1std) --
                 stays open through 80, closes at the forced 85 ceiling.
    nested:      does NOT clearly open until count=45 (10.5% ahead,
                 clears mean+1std) -- count=30 under nested is only
                 5.9% ahead and does NOT clear mean+1std. Stays open
                 from 45 through 80, closes at 85 same as independent.
  So "opens ~30, closes ~85" is accurate for independent construction
  specifically; under nested the knee opens roughly one count-step later
  (~45, not ~30). Both share the same closing behaviour (forced ceiling
  at 85). This is a genuine construction-dependent difference, not
  something the original report's independent-only table could show.

Q2 -- Does Hybrid still lose to Community12 on phase balance at 5 of 8
      counts, in both constructions, or was that a blend artifact?
  NEITHER, exactly -- two separate corrections:
    (a) It was not a blend artifact (Section 0) -- the prior report's
        table was already independent-only.
    (b) The "5 of 8" figure itself was WRONG even for independent
        construction: re-verified by code, Hybrid loses to Community12
        at 3 of 8 independent counts (30, 45, 49), not 5. The "5 of 8"
        number belonged to Community4, not Community12 -- the two counts
        were swapped in the prior report's prose (Section 0 above).
    (c) Under NESTED construction the finding is substantially
        DIFFERENT again: Hybrid loses to Community12 at only 1 of 8
        counts (80, by a mere 0.09pp) -- nested construction makes
        Hybrid's phase-balance advantage over Community12 far more
        consistent than independent construction does.
  Net: this finding is real but highly construction-dependent, and the
  original count was simply miscounted, independent of construction.

Q3 -- Resolve the count=15 discrepancy: which construction does each
      earlier-cited number belong to?
  5.520 (cited in the prior report and above, Section A3) is the
  INDEPENDENT value -- confirmed against reports/sweep_4a_tier1_report.txt's
  own Defect-1 table, which the source report's count=15 row itself
  frames as "independent/nested/diff": "15 | ... | 5.52 / 4.69 / -0.83"
  (post-fix column). 5.52 = independent, 4.69 = nested.
  4.686 (Section B3 above) is the NESTED value for the same count --
  matches that same source-report cell (4.69) to 2 decimal places.
  Both are real, sourced, and distinct -- 5.520 was never a blend of the
  two; it was always the independent figure, correctly labelled as such
  in the prior report's own header and never presented as anything else.
  IMPORTANT: the source report's OWN prose claim ("Hybrid beats Random's
  mean phase-deviation at 7 of 8 counts, all but 15") does not hold under
  EITHER construction -- Hybrid beats Random's mean at count=15 in BOTH
  independent (5.520 < 12.859) and nested (4.686 < 12.859). Checking both
  constructions this time makes that discrepancy (first flagged in the
  prior version of this report) more certain, not less: it is not
  explained by a construction mix-up either.
